# Axonis: visual, UX/UI and responsive upgrades (11 Oct 2026, pass 2)

Builds on `implementation-spec.md`, `motion-map.md` (MM) and `improvements-2026-10-11.md` (IMP). It does not repeat items IMP already ranked or shipped: real build progress, legend filters, depth/focus, ⌘K, Ask citations, list view, score breakdown, neutral cluster meters (IMP #11, still open: magenta meters are still live at 820 px, see `vr-clusters-820.png`). B² stays as it is: dark only, one accent `#FF2BD6` always paired with a second cue, Martian Mono + Mona Sans, radius 0, bracketed buttons, hairlines, spacing 8/16/24/32/48, no gradients or glow.

Method: Playwright on https://axonis.app and the demo map `/project/07796417-…` ("AI tutoring", 58 nodes, 56 edges, 10 clusters, 8 opportunities) at 1440×900, 820×1180 and 390×844. All requests were GET. I didn't open the Brief, didn't build, upload or delete. I opened the mobile inspector through search (tapping the canvas at random points missed every node, which is a finding in itself). References came from Mobbin and Refero MCP, the Motion docs MCP, the local Osmo vault mirror, and WebFetch of Magic UI, Motion Primitives, shadcn, bklit.com and manus.im. Screenshots are local (not committed) in `.playwright-mcp/vr-*.png`.

## 1. Critique per breakpoint

### 1440 px (desktop)
- **Home** (`vr-home-1440.png`). The composition works, but the headline (about 40 px) is small for a 1440 canvas. It and the sphere are two unrelated blocks: the sphere's centre sits about 60 px above the headline's optical centre, and the right third of the fold is empty. Nothing moves in sequence on arrival. The sphere spins, but the text is simply there.
- **Map** (`vr-map-1440.png`). The graph fills about 45% of the canvas, packed into the middle with large dead margins, so `fit` padding is too generous. **At fit zoom labels render at roughly 6 to 7 px** because `min-zoomed-font-size: 6` in `GraphCanvas.jsx:42` lets them shrink that far. They're legible only as texture. The dot grid is uniform and has no relation to the data, so the canvas reads as a flat void. **Inspector empty state**: a 300 px column holds one tiny `+` and two lines of 13 px grey text. It's the most expensive dead space on the screen.

### 820 px (tablet)
- **Home** (`vr-home-820.png`). Below 1100 px the hero stacks, so the sphere drops under the try-chips with about 250 px of empty space above it. On a portrait tablet the sphere is below the fold and the recent projects table is pushed further down.
- **Map** (`vr-map-820.png`). The desktop three-part layout is kept as is: the empty inspector column takes about 20% of the width and the graph is clipped at the left edge ("in Softwar…"). Controls and the ask bar sit at the canvas bottom, where they're fine for a tablet held in two hands.
- **Clusters** (`vr-clusters-820.png`). The 420 px cluster sidebar leaves a 380 px canvas, so the zoomed cluster is mostly off-screen. Meters are still magenta (IMP #11).

### 390 px (phone)
- **Home** (`vr-home-390.png`). Headline, query block and chips are well set. The sphere isn't visible above the fold at all, so the phone hero has no image.
- **Map** (`vr-map-390.png`). **The graph isn't fitted to the phone viewport**: labels run off the right edge ("Artificial Inte…"). The 56 px control column (+ − FIT DEPTH LIST) covers the left edge of the graph. Zoom buttons duplicate pinch, and DEPTH is greyed out. Tabs read `MAP · CLUST · OPPS`: abbreviations with no counts.
- **Node hit-testing**: five taps at plausible points didn't select a node. Nodes are 6 to 10 px on screen with no enlarged touch hit area.
- **Inspector sheet** (`vr-sheet-390.png`). It opens at about 64 dvh, so the selected node is hidden behind the sheet and the canvas behind it is dimmed by a backdrop. The 40×3 px drag handle looks draggable but nothing snaps. The content is good (title, Degree/Bridge cell bars, `[ ASK ABOUT THIS ]`).
- **Search** (`vr-search-390.png`). It works well, but the input is at the top of the screen, out of thumb reach, and the trigger is a 44 px icon in the top bar.
- **Opportunities** (`vr-opps-390.png`). The cards are strong typographically (96/100). One card fills more than a screen. IMP #12 covers density.

## 3. Considered and rejected

- **Gradient-masked backgrounds** (Magic UI Animated Grid Pattern with its radial fade, Aceternity spotlight or beams): gradients and glow break B². #10 keeps only the structure.
- **Constant scramble or flicker on hover** (Osmo Scramble Text Cursor, Magic UI Hyper Text on hover): motion that doesn't correspond to anything happening. #8 runs once on load, and that's all.
- **Motion+ AnimateNumber**: paid, and #12 is 30 lines of CSS.
- **Bottom tab bar as global app navigation**: Axonis has one workspace per project. A four-action rail (#4) beats a nav bar.
- **Vaul**: shadcn has moved its Drawer off Vaul to Base UI, and Vaul is no longer the maintained path. Use the hand-rolled sheet or Base UI.
