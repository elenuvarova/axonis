# KGE: B² element, screen and flow references

> The screenshots this file links to are third-party product screens (Mobbin, Refero, live sites). They stay out of this public repo; the same images sit on Figma page **06 · B² for KGE**.

Direction B²: off-black #0E0E0E, panels #171717, text #EDEDED, grey #7D7D7D, hairlines #2A2A2A, one magenta accent #FF2BD6, Martian Mono for UI and data, Mona Sans for long text, terminal feel, bracketed buttons, dense, sharp corners.

Rules followed (choosing-design-references): one ref per element, plus at most one alternate. Captions describe the mechanism. Element refs give only the composition inside the element. Screen refs give only the mass pattern. Colour, radius and type come from the B² tokens, never from these refs (Rule 10): most refs are rounded or light and are quoted only for structure.
Files are `.jpg` because that is the format the sources serve. They were not renamed to `.png`.

## Findings (Rule 7: thin catches are findings)

1. **Graph legend: no hit.** The Refero graph corpus is almost all Memotron, and no screen with a node-type legend came up (2 queries). Mobbin canvas results were node-editor tools (Railway, Writer, Magnific), also without legends. The legend has to be designed from a chart legend (dot + label), not quoted from a graph product.
2. **Bracketed buttons: one hit only** (Factory `[ enter to continue ]`), after 3 queries on Refero and Mobbin sections. The technique lives on brand and terminal marketing sites, not in app UIs. Treat it as a B² signature with a single ref.
3. **Inline-bar metric rows are rare in dark UIs.** Every usable catch (Google AI Studio, Gamma, Toggl) is light. Take the mechanism and apply the B² colours.
4. **Graph on mobile: none found.** Mobile canvas + sheet refs are all maps (Oura, Slopes, Zesty). The selected-object peek sheet transfers; the graph gestures have no ref.
5. **The counter refs are stills.** No source shows odometer motion. The rolling-digit animation needs a motion ref from another surface (web or motion libraries).
6. **Many strong mechanisms come in light or rounded skins** (Cofounder inspector, Gemini Notebook citations, Hers score card, the Perplexity flows). They are quoted for structure only. Corners and colour come from B², not from the refs.
7. **The dark upload flow is thin.** Both upload flows (Perplexity, ChatGPT) are light. The dark per-file status comes from element 18 (Revolut).
8. **Principal layout is still open.** Railway shows up in 4 slots (top bar alt, canvas controls, log stream, empty state) and also ships the counters/usage screens. It is the strongest candidate to measure as the one-app principal (5–8 screens, Rule 1). The ladder has not been measured here.
9. Aside (not requested): Unseen Studio's hero (Mobbin section `64611226-7921-4e34-9001-83c7e91bd803`) shows a wireframe sphere behind a wordmark. It came up by chance while searching for bracketed buttons and may be a starting point for the letter-sphere hero hunt.
